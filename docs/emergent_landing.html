02 — How it works

Five quiet steps from raw papers to ranked gaps.

Each stage is independent — swap one out, the rest keep working. The pipeline already runs end to end on two real corpora.

Step 01

Harvest the corpus

ACL Anthology, arXiv CS-CL, Semantic Scholar, and CORD-19 are collected and sampled to 200 papers per year per domain — 1,400 NLP papers and 840 COVID papers, 2018–2024.

Step 02

Extract entities & relations

SciBERT fine-tuned on SciERC reads each abstract and produces typed entities (Method, Task, Material, Metric, Other) plus relation labels (USED_FOR, ENTITY_ASSOCIATED_WITH_ENTITY, METHOD_APPLIED_TO, METHOD_EVALUATED_BY). 28,463 entities and 22,690 relations across the NLP corpus.

Step 03

Normalize to canonical nodes

Surface forms collapse onto canonical IDs via type-scoped fuzzy matching and abbreviation rules. One node per real concept, with a full provenance chain back to every paper that mentioned it.

Step 04

Embed per year, two channels

Node2vec walks the yearly co-occurrence graph; SPECTER2 pulls citation-context embeddings. Both are aligned per entity per year, giving a 128-dim position for every concept at every timestep.

Step 05

Score & validate gaps

Unconnected pairs with rising fused similarity are ranked by a priority function that folds in citation velocity. Retrospective cutoffs test whether the signal predicts future co-mentions — the 51% NLP hit rate is the proof.

03 — Proof

We cut the timeline and asked: did the gaps show up?

Retro-fitted validation is the only honest test for a gap detector — you can't wait five years to see if it worked. So we pretended it was 2021.

53% NLP hit rate

Of 75 top-ranked gaps scored with data ≤ 2021, 40 were co-mentioned in at least one paper from 2022–2024. The method isn't shouting — it's pointing at something.

20% COVID hit rate

Same procedure on the COVID corpus: 15 of 75 candidates materialized. Lower signal-to-noise, as expected for a faster-moving, more fragmented literature — but still well above random.

Why these numbers matter: a random pair from the concept space has near-zero probability of being co-mentioned in a future paper by chance. A 53% hit rate on the top 75 means the ranking function is concentrating probability mass where the literature is actually going. The COVID number is lower because the corpus is smaller and the topics more diffuse, but the direction is the same. This is a first validation — the pipeline is designed to improve as more years of post-cutoff data accumulate.
